About SOLARIS

We're on a mission to make clean solar energy accessible and affordable for every home and business.

Our Story

A Decade of Solar Excellence

Founded in 2014, SOLARIS began with a simple belief: everyone deserves access to clean, affordable energy. What started as a small team of passionate engineers has grown into one of the nation's most trusted solar energy providers.

Over the past 12 years, we've helped more than 500 families and businesses transition to solar power, reducing millions of pounds of carbon emissions and saving our clients over $50 million in energy costs.

Our commitment to quality, transparency, and customer satisfaction drives everything we do — from the initial consultation to ongoing system monitoring and support.
