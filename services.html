FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do solar panels last?
+
Modern solar panels typically last 25-30 years or more. Most manufacturers offer 25-year performance warranties, guaranteeing at least 80-90% output after 25 years. With proper maintenance, panels continue generating power well beyond their warranty period.
What incentives are available for going solar?
+
The federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC) currently offers a 30% tax credit on solar installation costs. Many states and utilities offer additional rebates, tax credits, and performance-based incentives. Our team helps you identify and maximize all available incentives.
How much can I save with solar panels?
+
Savings vary based on your energy usage, location, and system size, but most homeowners save 50-80% on their electricity bills. Over the lifetime of the system, the average savings exceed $25,000-$50,000 depending on your utility rates.
Do solar panels work on cloudy days?
+
Yes, solar panels generate electricity on cloudy days, though at reduced efficiency (typically 10-25% of peak output). Germany, which receives less sunlight than most US states, is one of the world's largest solar energy producers. With battery storage, you can store excess energy from sunny days for cloudy periods.
How long does installation take?
+
Residential installations typically take 1-3 days. Commercial installations may take 1-4 weeks depending on system size and complexity. The permitting and utility approval process can take 2-6 weeks before installation begins.
